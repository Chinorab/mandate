Privacy policy

Last updated [DATE]. This policy explains what Mandate records when you authorize an agent to shop for you, and what happens to that record.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name and email address.
  • Mandates. The instructions you write, the terms you sign, the time you signed, any changes you approve, and a fingerprint of the signed record.
  • Checks. Each cart your agent submits, the result for every term, and when the check ran.
  • Order references. The PayPal transaction ID, amount, merchant and order status for purchases made under a mandate.

We never receive your card or bank details. Payment happens inside PayPal.

How we use it

  • To stop a purchase before payment when it falls outside your mandate.
  • To give you receipts and a history of what your agent did.
  • To let a merchant answer a dispute with the records for that order.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Who can see it

  • You, for every mandate and check on your account.
  • The merchant of an order, for the mandate and check attached to that order only.
  • PayPal, which processes your payment under its own privacy statement.
  • The agent you connect, which receives the terms it must shop within.
  • Service providers that host and run Mandate for us: [LIST OF PROVIDERS].

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep mandates, checks and order references for [RETENTION PERIOD] after a mandate expires or is revoked, so the record is available if a dispute is opened. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your choices and rights

  • Revoke any mandate at any time. Your agent stops immediately.
  • Download the receipt for any mandate.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information.

Depending on the state you live in, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. We will not treat you differently for using them. Mandate is not intended for anyone under 18.

Security

Signed mandates are fingerprinted so any later change is detectable. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. No method is perfect, and we will notify you of a breach as the law requires.
